## Preamble

The three Parties are the maintainers of the Shimera project. The Shimera code is a collaborative work (*œuvre de collaboration*, French Intellectual Property Code, articles L113-2 and L113-3) co-owned by its authors, who must exercise their rights by common agreement.

The contribution grant in `CONTRIBUTING.md` gives the maintainers the right to relicense the contributions of external contributors, but it does not say how the maintainers decide among themselves. This agreement fills that gap, as required by section 3 of the [Legal Protection Strategy](./legal_protection_strategy.md).

The Parties therefore agree as follows.

## Article 1 - Purpose

This agreement sets out how the Maintainers make Re-licensing Decisions about the Project, and what happens when they disagree, when one of them does not respond, leaves the project, or when a new maintainer joins.

## Article 2 - Definitions

- **Project**: the Shimera library and everything distributed with it under the same license (source code, shaders, tests, examples, documentation).
- **Maintainers**: the Parties to this agreement, and any person who later joins it under Article 7.
- **Contributors**: any person other than a Maintainer who contributes to the Project under the terms of `CONTRIBUTING.md`.
- **Re-licensing Decision**: any decision to:
  - replace the Project's current license (GPL-3.0-only) with another license;
  - add a license alongside the current one (dual licensing);
  - grant a commercial or proprietary license on all or part of the Project to a third party.

## Article 3 - Decision process

1. **Proposal**: any Maintainer can submit a Re-licensing Decision proposal. The proposal is made in writing (email or a written message on the team's usual channel) and is sent to every Maintainer. It states the proposed license or terms, the parts of the Project concerned, and the reasons.
2. **Discussion period**: the Maintainers have **14 days** from the date the proposal is sent to discuss it and vote.
3. **Vote**: each Maintainer has **one vote**: for, against, or abstention. Votes are given in writing.
4. **Record**: the result of the vote, the date, and each Maintainer's vote are written down and kept with this agreement.

## Article 4 - Disagreement and majority rule

1. **Majority**: a proposal is adopted when it receives **more votes for than votes against**. Abstentions are not counted.
2. **Majority decision prevails**: in the event of a disagreement, the majority decision prevails. No Maintainer has a veto.
3. **Binding effect**: a decision adopted under this article binds every Maintainer, including those who voted against it or abstained. Each Maintainer commits to:
   - not opposing or blocking the decision once it is adopted;
   - signing any document needed to carry it out (license notices, agreements with third parties, etc.).
4. **Dissent**: a Maintainer who voted against the decision can ask for their disagreement and their reasons to be written down in the record.
5. **Tie**: if the votes for and against are equal, the proposal is rejected and the current license stays in place. The same proposal can be submitted again later.

## Article 5 - No response

1. If a Maintainer has not voted by the end of the discussion period, any other Maintainer sends them a **written reminder**, which gives them **7 more days** to vote.
2. If they still have not voted after this reminder, their silence counts as an **abstention**, and the majority is counted among the Maintainers who voted.

## Article 6 - Leaving the project

1. A Maintainer can leave the Project at any time by informing the other Maintainers in writing.
2. The departing Maintainer **keeps the copyright** on their own contributions.
3. The departing Maintainer's **consent to Re-licensing Decisions remains in force**: they authorize the remaining Maintainers to make Re-licensing Decisions about the Project, including their contributions, under this agreement.
4. The departing Maintainer **loses their vote** from the date they leave.

## Article 7 - New maintainers

A person who becomes a maintainer of the Project only gets a vote after signing this agreement. They are then a Maintainer with the same rights and obligations as the original Parties.

## Article 8 - Limits

1. **Released versions stay under their license**: a Re-licensing Decision only applies to future versions. Versions already released under the GPL-3.0-only license remain available under that license, which cannot be withdrawn.
2. **External Contributors**: the rights of Contributors are governed by `CONTRIBUTING.md`, not by this agreement.
3. **No transfer of copyright**: this agreement does not transfer any copyright between the Maintainers. Each Maintainer keeps the copyright on their own contributions.

## Article 9 - Amendment

This agreement can only be amended with the **written agreement of all Maintainers**. Majority rule (Article 4) does not apply to changes to this agreement.

## Article 10 - Governing law and jurisdiction

This agreement is governed by **French law**. The Parties will first try to settle any dispute about this agreement amicably. If they cannot, the dispute is brought before the competent French courts.
